# Requirements: Gravity Shift

Gravity Shift is the GameDNA demo platformer (PRD section 11, App A): an atmospheric puzzle platformer in the layered-minimal style. The player traverses obstacles, reverses gravity, collects keys and reaches the exit. Protected constraints in `.gamedna/project.json` link to the IDs below.

## REQ-1: Core loop and scope

**User story:** As a player, I want short levels where I move, jump and reverse gravity to collect keys and reach a door.

1. The game SHALL have a title screen, a tutorial hint per level, three short levels, pause, restart, a win state and a reduced-motion setting.
2. WHEN the player stands on a surface and presses Shift or G THEN gravity SHALL reverse.
3. All art and sound SHALL be generated by GameDNA from recorded seeds (`.gamedna/assets-manifest.json`).

## REQ-2: No repeated midair jump (MOVE-01)

1. WHEN the player has jumped since last touching a surface THEN a further jump press SHALL NOT start another jump.
2. Coyote time SHALL apply only after walking off a ledge, never after a jump.

## REQ-3: No input while dead (DEAD-01)

1. WHILE the player is dead THE game SHALL ignore movement, jump and gravity input until respawn.

## REQ-4: Restart resets the level (RESET-01)

1. WHEN the player presses R THEN keys, position, velocity and gravity SHALL return to the level's initial state.

## REQ-5: Exit requires keys (WIN-01)

1. WHEN the player reaches the exit without every key THEN the level SHALL NOT complete and the game SHALL say the door is sealed.
2. WHEN the player reaches the exit with every key THEN the level SHALL complete exactly once.
