# Requirements: Neon Survivor

Neon Survivor is the GameDNA demo arcade game (PRD section 11, App B): a neon geometric top-down survival game. The player moves, evades and pulse-attacks enemies and survives escalating waves. Protected constraints in `.gamedna/project.json` link to the IDs below.

## REQ-1: Core loop and scope

1. The game SHALL have a title screen, one arena, five waves with three enemy types, health, score, restart, victory and game over.
2. Combat feedback (hit-stop, knockback, flash, shake, sparks) SHALL be tunable; shake and flashes SHALL turn off with reduced motion.
3. All art and sound SHALL be generated by GameDNA from recorded seeds.

## REQ-2: Cooldown cannot be bypassed (CD-01)

1. WHEN the attack key is pressed or held THEN consecutive pulse attacks SHALL be at least `attack.cooldownMs` apart.

## REQ-3: A defeated enemy scores once (SCORE-01)

1. WHEN an enemy is defeated THEN exactly one score event SHALL be emitted for that enemy.

## REQ-4: Health is never negative (HP-01)

1. WHEN the player takes damage at zero health THEN health SHALL stay at zero and the run SHALL end.

## REQ-5: Restart clears run state (RESET-01)

1. WHEN the player presses R THEN score, wave, health and enemies SHALL return to their starting values.
